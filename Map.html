🆘 Help & Guide

🗺️ Navigation Tips

• Click any colored area on the map to view room details

• Hover over areas to see tooltips

• Use the search box to quickly find locations

📊 Your Progress

Track your exploration at the top of the page:

• Rooms Explored: Unique rooms you've clicked

• Total Clicks: How many times you've interacted

• Time Spent: Duration on the map

💡 Pro Tip: Try to explore all rooms to get familiar with the campus layout!

🧭 Pathfinding Feature

Use the "Find Your Way" tool to get directions:

• Select your current location from the first dropdown

• Select your destination from the second dropdown

• Click "Show Path" to see an animated route

• Green marker = Start, Red pulsing marker = Destination

• Click "Reset" to clear the path and start over

📱 Mobile Users

Pinch to zoom on the map for better viewing on small screens.
